## Introduction
First, this document will describe the construction of three-phase motors. Two-phase or one-phase motors will not be addressed here, as they are far less common in BLDCs.

It is important to consider and understand the difference between different mechanical configurations and winding parameters, such as:

1. Mechanical Attributes (stator and PM rotor magnet construction)
2. Winding attributes: Distributed vs. concentrated windings
3. Single and double-layer windings
4. Series vs. parallel windings,
5. Delta configuration vs star (Wye) termination,
6. Winding factor (kw),
