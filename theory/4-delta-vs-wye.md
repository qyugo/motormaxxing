# Delta vs. Wye Configuration

Unlike a single-phase brushed motor, most BLDCs involve three distinct motor phases that are wired in a 120 degree orientation. 
The pattern in which the three-phase coil windings are wired on a motor can adjust its performance. Star configurations are generally suitable for low-speed applications, as its efficiency is higher at a low RPM. Delta configurations provide better torque efficiency at a high speed and high power. Star configurations involve a neutral point, and less phase current, while delta configurations involve higher phase current and thus higher heat concentration.
