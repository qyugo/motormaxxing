## FEMM Simulations

A Finite Element Method for Magnetics program was used to simulate the 2D behavior of the stator and rotor cross sections after exporting a drawing of the stator from Onshape, and generating winding and magnet configrations, airgap length, rotor material and thickness using Python.
Here, you can find results for the current V2 (PETG) and V3 (1018 Steel) models. 

A mesh and B-field (magnetic flux density, in Tesla) was generated along with a plot of airgap flux density (Bg in my analytical model) so that I can compare the two later on.

Further, a plot for cogging torque, no-load flux, and loaded torque at 5A and 10A was generated using a sweep of the rotor across 360 degrees, having split the stator and rotor groups separately.
